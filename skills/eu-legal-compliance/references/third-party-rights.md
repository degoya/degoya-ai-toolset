# Third-party rights

## Copyright (DE: UrhG; AT: UrhG)
- [ ] For every image, video, icon, font, music track and third-party text, origin and licence are documented (source, licence type, date, scope of use).
- [ ] Stock photos: licence covers the use (web, app, advertising, print, social media, number of users).
- [ ] Attribution where the licence requires it (e.g. CC BY), in the required form. In Germany, photographers also have a statutory right to be named (§ 13 UrhG).
- [ ] Web fonts: licence allows web/app embedding.
- [ ] Quotations only for a referencing purpose, with source, in the required extent.
- [ ] Screenshots of third-party software/websites: check purpose.
- [ ] AI-generated content: respect the generator's terms; check output for recognisable copies of third-party works, trademarks or persons. Purely AI-generated content is generally not protected by copyright in the EU.

## Trademarks (national law, EU Trade Mark Regulation)
- [ ] Product, app and domain names checked against registers (DPMA, EUIPO, WIPO) in the relevant classes.
- [ ] Third-party marks/logos only descriptively and without likelihood of confusion; brand guidelines (e.g. app-store badges) respected.
- [ ] No misleading use of third-party marks as keywords/meta.

## Image and personality rights (DE: KUG; GDPR)
- [ ] Recognisable persons only with consent (model release); exceptions are narrow.
- [ ] Employee photos: written consent, withdrawal considered.
- [ ] User-generated content: usage rights granted in terms; notice-and-action mechanism (DSA Art. 16).
- [ ] Deepfakes/realistic AI images of persons: see ai-labelling.md.

## Software and open source
- [ ] Dependencies checked for licences – see skill `license-check`.
- [ ] Code snippets from forums/AI assistants: respect licences (Stack Overflow: CC BY-SA).

## Unfair competition (DE: UWG; EU UCPD)
- [ ] No misleading claims ("No. 1", "best", "free" with hidden costs); comparative advertising only within legal limits.
- [ ] Environmental claims ("climate neutral", "green") only if substantiated and specific.
- [ ] Reviews: state whether and how authenticity is checked; no bought or fake reviews.
