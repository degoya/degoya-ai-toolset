# Mandatory information and legally sound wording

## Imprint / legal notice (EU E-Commerce Directive Art. 5; DE: § 5 DDG, formerly TMG; § 18 MStV for editorial content; AT: § 5 ECG, § 25 MedienG; CH: Art. 3 UWG)
- [ ] Reachable from every page and inside apps, max. 2 clicks, clearly labelled ("Impressum" in DE/AT).
- [ ] Name/company, legal form, authorised representatives, address for service (no P.O. box).
- [ ] Fast electronic contact (email) plus a second communication channel.
- [ ] Register and registration number, VAT ID, supervisory authority, chamber, professional title where applicable.
- [ ] Editorial content (DE): person responsible under § 18(2) MStV.

## Consumer law (B2C)
- [ ] Withdrawal information and model withdrawal form; from 19 June 2026 additionally an electronic **withdrawal function** ("withdraw from contract" button) for contracts concluded online (Directive (EU) 2023/2673; check the national implementation).
- [ ] Order button labelled e.g. "order with obligation to pay" (DE: "zahlungspflichtig bestellen", § 312j BGB), essential characteristics directly above.
- [ ] Cancellation button for subscriptions (DE: § 312k BGB).
- [ ] Prices incl. VAT, shipping costs, unit price; price reductions with the lowest price of the previous 30 days (Omnibus Directive; DE: § 11 PAngV).
- [ ] EU ODR platform link: obsolete since the platform was shut down on 20 July 2025 – remove it; check consumer dispute resolution statement (DE: § 36 VSBG).
- [ ] Terms: understandable, no surprising clauses; jurisdiction clauses only B2B.

## Accessibility (European Accessibility Act; DE: BFSG, since 28 June 2025)
- [ ] In scope: e.g. e-commerce, banking services, e-books, passenger transport for consumers. Micro-enterprises (< 10 employees and ≤ €2 million turnover/balance sheet) exempt for services.
- [ ] Provide accessibility information/statement. Technical review: skill `accessibility`.

## Digital Services Act (platforms/hosting third-party content)
- [ ] Points of contact (Art. 11/12), notice-and-action (Art. 16), statement of reasons for moderation (Art. 17), transparency in terms (Art. 14), no dark patterns (Art. 25).

## Wording rules for legal texts and UI notices
- Clear and understandable for the audience (Art. 12 GDPR: "concise, transparent, intelligible and easily accessible form, using clear and plain language").
- No downplaying ("we only use a few cookies"), no threats, no dark patterns.
- Avoid absolute claims that cannot be proven ("100 % secure", "GDPR certified" without a certificate).
- Consistent terms across banner, privacy notice and settings page.
- Legal texts in every offered language; define the authoritative language version.
