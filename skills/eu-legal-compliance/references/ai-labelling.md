# AI labelling and the EU AI Act

Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 (AI Act). In force since 1 August 2024, applied in stages. Deadlines and details are being adjusted through guidelines, codes of practice and possible amendments (e.g. the "Digital Omnibus"): **check the current state before every report.**

## Timeline (as in the regulation text)
- 2 February 2025: prohibited practices (Art. 5), AI literacy of staff (Art. 4)
- 2 August 2025: obligations for providers of general-purpose AI models, governance, penalties
- 2 August 2026: most obligations, including **transparency obligations under Art. 50** and high-risk systems under Annex III
- 2 August 2027: high-risk systems as safety components under Annex I

## Determine the role
- **Provider:** develops an AI system (or has it developed) and places it on the market under its own name.
- **Deployer:** uses an AI system under its own authority (e.g. a website with a third-party chatbot).

## Determine the risk class
1. **Prohibited (Art. 5)?** e.g. manipulative techniques, social scoring, emotion recognition at work/in education, untargeted scraping of facial images.
2. **High-risk (Art. 6, Annex III)?** e.g. recruitment, creditworthiness, access to education or essential services. Extensive obligations: refer to a professional.
3. **Transparency obligations (Art. 50)?** typical for websites and apps, see below.
4. **Minimal risk:** no specific obligations, voluntary codes.

## Transparency obligations (Art. 50)
| Case | Obligation | Who |
|---|---|---|
| AI interacts directly with people (chatbot, voicebot) | Inform people that they are interacting with AI, unless obvious | Provider |
| AI generates synthetic audio, image, video or text | Mark outputs in a machine-readable way as artificially generated (e.g. watermark, metadata, C2PA) | Provider |
| Emotion recognition or biometric categorisation | Inform the persons exposed | Deployer |
| Deepfakes (realistic image, audio, video content that appears authentic) | Disclose that the content is artificially generated or manipulated; limited for art/satire | Deployer |
| AI-generated text published to inform the public on matters of public interest | Disclose, unless human review/editorial responsibility | Deployer |

The information must be given clearly and distinguishably, at the latest at the first interaction or exposure, and must be accessible.

## Checklist for websites and apps
- [ ] Chatbot/assistant: notice in the chat window before or with the first message.
- [ ] AI-generated images/videos of realistic-looking people, places, events: visible label on the content.
- [ ] Generated media keep metadata/watermarks (not stripped on upload/compression).
- [ ] AI features described in the privacy notice (provider, data flow, purpose).
- [ ] Staff using AI systems are trained (Art. 4, AI literacy).
- [ ] Platform rules additionally respected (app stores, Google, Meta, YouTube require their own AI labels).

## Wording suggestions (adapt to the language of the offering)
- Chatbot (EN): "You are chatting with an AI assistant. Answers may contain errors. For binding information, please contact …"
- Chatbot (DE): "Sie chatten mit einem KI-Assistenten. Antworten können Fehler enthalten. Für verbindliche Auskünfte wenden Sie sich an …"
- Image: "AI-generated" / "Edited with AI" (DE: "KI-generiert" / "Mit KI bearbeitet") on the image or in the caption.
- Article: "This text was created with AI assistance and editorially reviewed."

Penalties for breaching Art. 50: up to €15 million or 3 % of worldwide annual turnover (Art. 99).
