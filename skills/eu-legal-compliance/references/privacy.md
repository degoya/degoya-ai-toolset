# Privacy: GDPR and ePrivacy/TDDDG

## Principles (Art. 5 GDPR)
Lawfulness, transparency, purpose limitation, data minimisation, accuracy, storage limitation, integrity/confidentiality, accountability.

## Checklist
**Legal basis (Art. 6, special categories Art. 9)**
- [ ] Every processing activity has a named legal basis (consent, contract, legal obligation, legitimate interest with balancing test).
- [ ] Consent is freely given, informed, unambiguous, provable and as easy to withdraw as to give (Art. 7).

**Terminal equipment access / cookies (Art. 5(3) ePrivacy Directive; DE: § 25 TDDDG)**
- [ ] Non-essential cookies, local storage, pixels, fingerprinting only **after** consent.
- [ ] Banner: "Reject" equivalent to "Accept" on the first layer, no pre-ticked boxes, no nudging.
- [ ] Settings can be reopened at any time (footer link).
- [ ] In code: are scripts loaded before consent? (Google Fonts, Maps, YouTube, Analytics, Meta Pixel, Hotjar etc.) Self-host web fonts.

**Transparency (Art. 12–14)**
- [ ] Privacy notice easy to reach (max. 2 clicks, footer), in the language of the offering.
- [ ] Contents: controller, DPO if any, purposes and legal bases, recipients, international transfers, retention, data subject rights, right to lodge a complaint, whether provision is required, automated decision-making.
- [ ] Forms: notice at the form, required fields limited to what is necessary.

**Processors and international transfers (Art. 28, 44 ff.)**
- [ ] Data processing agreement (DPA) with every service provider processing personal data (hosting, email, CRM, analytics, AI APIs).
- [ ] Third-country transfers: adequacy decision (e.g. EU-US Data Privacy Framework – check provider certification) or standard contractual clauses plus transfer impact assessment.

**Data subject rights (Art. 15–22)**
- [ ] Process for access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, objection; deadline 1 month.
- [ ] Apps: account deletion possible inside the app (also an Apple/Google store requirement).

**Technical and organisational measures (Art. 25, 32)**
- [ ] Privacy by design/default: data-minimising defaults.
- [ ] Encryption (TLS, at rest), access concept, logs without unnecessary personal data, IP truncation.
- [ ] Deletion concept with retention periods.

**Documentation**
- [ ] Record of processing activities (Art. 30).
- [ ] DPIA (Art. 35) for high risk – see skill `dsfa`.
- [ ] Breach notification process, 72 hours (Art. 33).

**AI features with personal data**
- [ ] Are inputs sent to external AI providers? DPA, third country, training on customer data excluded?
- [ ] Described in the privacy notice; consent if required.
- [ ] No solely automated decisions with legal effect without an Art. 22 basis.

Sanctions: fines up to €20 million or 4 % of worldwide annual turnover (Art. 83); plus cease-and-desist letters and damages (Art. 82).
